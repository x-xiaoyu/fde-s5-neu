MERIDIAN ANALYTICS GROUP — EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Section 7: Business Expenses
Last Revised: March 2025

7.1 General Policy
Meridian Analytics Group reimburses employees for reasonable, documented business expenses
incurred while performing work duties. All expenses require an original itemized receipt and
must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred. Expenses submitted after 30 days may be
denied at manager discretion and will require VP-level approval to be processed. Expenses
submitted more than 90 days after the date incurred will not be reimbursed under any
circumstances.

Employees are expected to exercise the same judgment spending company funds as they would
their own. Lavish or excessive spending, even within stated limits, may result in denial of
reimbursement or disciplinary action.

7.2 Air Travel
Economy class airfare is reimbursable for all flights with a total flight time under 6 hours.
Premium economy is permissible for flights of 6 to 9 hours with director-level approval.
Business class is permissible for flights of 9 hours or more with VP-level approval. First
class is not reimbursable under any circumstances.

Employees should book flights at least 14 days in advance when possible to take advantage
of lower fares. Refundable fares require VP approval. Airfare must be booked through Concur
Travel to access corporate negotiated rates; bookings made outside Concur may be reimbursed
at the Concur equivalent fare for that route and date, with the employee responsible for
any difference.

7.3 Hotel and Lodging
Hotel stays are reimbursable at actual cost up to the following nightly limits (excluding
taxes and fees): $200 in standard markets, $275 in elevated markets (Boston, Chicago,
Seattle, Washington D.C., Los Angeles), and $350 in high-cost markets (New York City,
San Francisco, London, Zurich, Tokyo). Employees may request a high-cost market designation
for unlisted cities by submitting a market rate verification to Finance.

Employees should use the corporate Concur portal for hotel bookings where possible.
Loyalty program points and rewards accrued during company travel belong to the employee.
Hotel incidentals charged to the room beyond the nightly room rate (minibar, movies,
spa services) are not reimbursable.

7.4 Meals and Dining
Meal reimbursements are capped at $60 per day for domestic travel and $90 per day for
international travel. These per-diem limits cover all meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner)
and non-alcoholic beverages for the travel day. Receipts are required for any individual
meal over $25.

Business meals with clients, prospects, or external partners are reimbursable up to $150
per person with manager approval. The expense report must include: the name and employer of
each attendee, the business purpose of the meal, and the restaurant name and date.

Alcohol is reimbursable as part of a client meal but may not exceed 20% of the pre-tax
food total. Alcohol purchased outside of a client meal context is not reimbursable.

7.5 Ground Transportation
Rideshare services (Uber, Lyft) and taxis to and from airports, client sites, and company
offices are reimbursable with receipt. Personal vehicle use for business purposes is
reimbursed at the current IRS standard mileage rate, published on the Finance intranet at
the start of each calendar year. Parking fees and tolls are reimbursable with receipt.

Rental cars require manager approval and must use economy or compact class unless no
vehicles of those classes are available at the pickup location. Rental car insurance offered
by the rental agency should be declined, as Meridian maintains a corporate travel insurance
policy. Personal vehicle rentals that use the employee's personal credit card may not be
covered under the corporate policy; employees should use the corporate card for rentals.

7.6 Expense Submission Process
All expenses must be submitted via Concur Expense within 30 days of the expenditure date.
Itemized receipts must be attached for all expenses over $25. Expenses under $25 require
written documentation of the business purpose but may be submitted without a receipt.
Manager approval is required for any single expense over $500; VP approval is required
for any single expense over $2,000.

Expense reports are reviewed within 5 business days of submission. Approved reports are
processed within 10 business days of approval and reimbursed via direct deposit on the
next regular payroll cycle following processing. Disputed or returned expense items will
be communicated via Concur with a reason code; employees may resubmit with additional
documentation.

7.7 International Travel
Employees traveling internationally must notify HR and Finance at least 2 weeks prior to
departure, regardless of trip duration. Per diem rates for international locations follow
U.S. State Department guidelines and are published on the Finance intranet at the start of
each calendar year.

Currency conversion for reimbursement will be applied at the interbank exchange rate on
the date of expenditure, using the rate published by the European Central Bank or
equivalent. Employees are strongly encouraged to use the Meridian corporate card for
international expenses to avoid currency conversion markup and to simplify expense
documentation.

Visa fees, passport renewal fees required for business travel, and required vaccinations
or travel health consultations are reimbursable with documentation. Travel insurance
purchased independently is not reimbursable as Meridian maintains a corporate policy.

7.8 Technology and Equipment
Software subscriptions required for work purposes may be expensed with manager approval
up to $50 per month without a formal procurement request. Subscriptions over $50 per month
or any hardware purchase require a formal IT procurement request submitted through the
IT Service Portal. Personal devices (phones, laptops, tablets) are not reimbursable even
if used for work purposes; employees requiring a device should submit a request through IT.

Home office equipment for remote employees (monitors, keyboards, webcams) is reimbursable
up to $500 per calendar year with manager and HR approval. This allowance resets on
January 1st and does not carry over.
