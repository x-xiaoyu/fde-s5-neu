MERIDIAN ANALYTICS GROUP — IT SYSTEMS AND ACCESS POLICY
Section 9: Technology Use, Access Management, and Security
Last Revised: April 2025

9.1 Acceptable Use of Company Systems
Company-owned or company-managed technology resources — including laptops, mobile devices,
software, networks, email, and cloud services — are provided for business purposes. Incidental
personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work responsibilities, consume
significant bandwidth or storage, expose company systems to security risk, or violate any
provision of this or other Meridian policies.

Prohibited uses include but are not limited to: accessing, storing, or distributing illegal
content; running personal business activities on company systems; using company email or
systems to engage in harassment or discriminatory conduct; installing unauthorized software
that has not been approved by the IT department; and circumventing security controls,
monitoring tools, or access restrictions.

Employees have no expectation of privacy when using company systems. Meridian reserves the
right to monitor, access, and retain records of all activity on company-owned or managed
systems for security, compliance, and operational purposes.

9.2 Access Management
Access to Meridian systems and data is granted on the principle of least privilege: employees
receive the minimum access required to perform their job functions. Access is provisioned by
IT upon receipt of a formal access request approved by the employee's manager.

Access rights are reviewed quarterly by IT and department managers. Employees who change
roles within the company must have their access rights updated within 5 business days of
the role change effective date. Access that is no longer required must be revoked promptly.

All employee access is disabled on the last day of employment. Former employees retain no
rights to access company systems after separation, regardless of the nature of their departure.
Employees must not retain copies of company data after their last day.

9.3 Password and Authentication Requirements
All Meridian system accounts must use passwords that are at least 14 characters in length
and include a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special
characters. Passwords must not include the employee's name, username, or common dictionary
words. Passwords must be changed every 180 days.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for all systems accessible outside the
corporate network, including email, VPN, cloud storage, and any SaaS platform integrated
with Meridian SSO. Employees must enroll in MFA within their first 5 business days.
Requests for MFA exemptions will not be granted.

Employees must not share passwords or authentication credentials with any other person,
including their own manager or IT staff. The IT help desk will never ask for a password.

9.4 Device Management
All devices used to access Meridian systems or data — whether company-owned or personal
(BYOD) — must have the Meridian Mobile Device Management (MDM) profile installed. MDM
enables the company to enforce security settings, remotely wipe lost or stolen devices,
and ensure compliance with security standards.

Company-issued laptops must not be used by family members or others outside the employee's
household. Employees traveling internationally must notify IT at least 5 business days before
departure; additional security configurations may be required for travel to certain countries.

If a device is lost or stolen, the employee must notify IT Security immediately. IT may
perform a remote wipe of the device. The employee is responsible for reporting the loss
to their manager and completing an IT incident form within 24 hours.

9.5 Software Installation and Shadow IT
Only software that has been approved and listed on the IT-maintained Software Catalog may
be installed on Meridian devices. Requests for new software must be submitted through the
IT Service Portal and include a business justification. Standard approval time is 5 to 7
business days. Urgent requests may be escalated to the IT Director.

Employees may not use unapproved software as a workaround or create unauthorized
technology solutions (Shadow IT) to solve business problems. Shadow IT creates security
vulnerabilities, compliance risks, and data management problems that are difficult to
detect and remediate. Employees who identify a business need not served by existing tools
should submit a formal software request to IT rather than implementing their own solution.

Cloud-based tools accessed through a browser (as opposed to installed software) are subject
to the same approval requirement. Using a personal Google or Microsoft account to store
company data, for example, is a Shadow IT violation.

9.6 Remote Work and VPN
Employees working remotely must connect to the Meridian VPN for any work that involves
accessing Tier 3 or Tier 4 data, internal systems, or client environments. Tier 1 and
Tier 2 work may be performed without VPN, but the VPN is recommended for all remote work
on public or untrusted networks (cafes, hotels, airports).

The VPN client is available for download from the IT intranet. Employees experiencing
connectivity issues should contact the IT help desk before attempting to troubleshoot
independently, as some self-remediation attempts can create security gaps.

9.7 Security Awareness
Employees must complete the annual Cybersecurity Awareness Training module in the Meridian
LMS by January 31st each year. New employees must complete it within their first 30 days.

Meridian conducts periodic simulated phishing campaigns. Employees who repeatedly fail
phishing simulations will be required to complete additional security training. Habitual
failure to recognize phishing attempts may result in escalation to HR.

Employees are encouraged to report suspicious emails, links, or activity to the IT Security
team at security@meridianag.com. Reporting suspicious activity is always the right course
of action, even if the concern turns out to be unfounded.

9.8 Help Desk and Support
The IT Help Desk is available Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM Eastern, and can
be reached by phone at ext. 4357 (HELP), by chat in the Meridian IT Teams channel, or by
submitting a ticket at helpdesk.meridianag.com. After-hours emergency support for critical
system outages is available by phone only.

Standard ticket response time is 4 business hours for Priority 3 (routine) requests and
1 business hour for Priority 2 (impaired productivity) requests. Priority 1 (critical
system down) incidents are addressed immediately upon receipt.
