MERIDIAN ANALYTICS GROUP — DATA GOVERNANCE POLICY
Section 12: Data Classification, Handling, and AI Tool Usage
Last Revised: February 2025

12.1 Purpose and Scope
This policy applies to all Meridian Analytics Group employees, contractors, vendors, and
any party who accesses Meridian data systems or handles Meridian data in any form. Its
purpose is to ensure that data is handled in a manner consistent with its sensitivity,
applicable law, and client contractual obligations. Violations of this policy may result
in disciplinary action up to and including termination and may expose the individual and
the company to legal liability.

12.2 Data Classification Tiers
All data at Meridian Analytics Group is classified into one of four tiers at the time of
creation or receipt. If classification is unclear, employees must treat data as Tier 3
until the Chief Data Officer or a designated Data Steward makes a formal classification
determination.

Tier 1 — Public
Information approved for unrestricted external distribution. Examples include published
research reports, press releases, marketing materials, job postings, and content on the
public Meridian website. No access controls are required for Tier 1 data, and it may be
freely shared inside and outside the organization.

Tier 2 — Internal
General business information not intended for external audiences, but whose loss or
unauthorized disclosure would not materially harm the company or its clients. Examples
include internal memos, meeting agendas, non-sensitive operational data, and general
project status updates. Tier 2 data should not be shared externally without manager
approval but does not require encryption at rest.

Tier 3 — Confidential
Sensitive business information whose unauthorized disclosure could harm the company,
its clients, or its employees. Examples include client deliverables and underlying data,
financial projections, personnel records and compensation information, proprietary
methodologies and frameworks, and vendor contracts. Access to Tier 3 data is role-based
and requires explicit manager or Data Steward approval. Tier 3 data may not be stored
on personal devices, personal cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud), or any
system not managed by Meridian IT.

Tier 4 — Restricted
Highly sensitive information whose unauthorized disclosure would cause severe harm.
Examples include active merger and acquisition activity, board-level financial data,
executive compensation, unreleased product roadmaps, regulatory filings prior to
submission, and any data subject to a legal hold. Access to Tier 4 data is limited to
named individuals approved by a VP or above and the Chief Data Officer. Tier 4 data
must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3 or higher. Tier 4
data may not be printed without CDO approval.

12.3 AI Tool Usage and Data Classification
The use of AI tools in the workplace is subject to the following restrictions based on
data classification tier.

Employees may use external AI tools (including but not limited to ChatGPT, Claude,
Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, or any third-party AI assistant or API) freely with
Tier 1 and Tier 2 data.

Employees may NOT input Tier 3 or Tier 4 data into any external AI tool without explicit
written approval from the Chief Data Officer. This prohibition applies regardless of
whether the AI provider claims not to use inputs for training. The risk of data transmission
to external servers, prompt logging, or future policy changes by the AI provider means
that confidential and restricted data must not leave Meridian-controlled systems.

Employees who need AI assistance with Tier 3 or Tier 4 work should submit a request to
the IT department, which maintains a portfolio of approved internal AI tools deployed
on Meridian-controlled infrastructure where data does not leave the corporate environment.

The following uses are explicitly prohibited regardless of data tier:
- Inputting client personal data (names, contact information, financial details) into
  external AI tools under any circumstances
- Using AI tools to draft communications that will be sent externally as if authored
  by the employee without review and editing
- Using AI tools to circumvent any access control, approval process, or policy

12.4 Data Retention
Tier 1 and Tier 2 data is retained for 3 years from the date of creation unless subject
to a legal hold or a client contractual requirement specifying a longer period.

Tier 3 data is retained for 7 years from the date of creation or last active use,
whichever is later. Client data is subject to the retention terms specified in the
applicable client contract, which supersedes this default policy.

Tier 4 data retention periods are defined individually by Legal and the Chief Data Officer
based on regulatory requirements and vary by data type.

Employees must not delete, alter, or destroy any data that is subject to a legal hold,
regardless of tier or normal retention schedule. Legal holds are communicated by the
Legal department and remain in effect until explicitly lifted in writing.

12.5 Incident Reporting
Any suspected data breach, unauthorized access, or unintentional disclosure of Tier 3 or
Tier 4 data must be reported to the Information Security team (security@meridianag.com)
within 4 hours of discovery, regardless of the perceived severity. The IS team will assess
the incident, determine notification obligations, and escalate per the Incident Response
Playbook.

Employees must also report any of the following as potential incidents: a lost or stolen
device that accessed Meridian data, a misdirected email containing Tier 3 or Tier 4 data,
a suspected phishing attack whether or not credentials were entered, and any unauthorized
third party who appears to have accessed Meridian systems.

Failure to report a known or suspected breach within the required timeframe is a terminable
offense. Employees who report incidents in good faith will not face adverse employment
action for the underlying incident provided they did not act with intent or gross negligence.

12.6 Third-Party Data Sharing
Client data (Tier 3) may only be shared with third parties — including contractors,
vendors, technology platforms, and partner firms — under a signed and current Data
Processing Agreement (DPA). Employees must not share client data with any third party
without first confirming with the Legal team that a current DPA is on file.

The Legal team maintains a DPA registry accessible on the company intranet under
Legal > Vendor Agreements > Data Processing Agreements. If a DPA is not on file for
a vendor with whom you need to share client data, contact Legal to initiate the agreement
process. Standard DPA execution time is 5 to 10 business days.

Sharing client data with a third party without a current DPA constitutes a breach of
client contract and Meridian policy, and may trigger mandatory breach notification
obligations under applicable law.

12.7 Employee Training and Acknowledgment
All employees must complete the annual Data Governance Training module in the Meridian
Learning Management System (LMS) by January 31st each year. New employees must complete
the training within their first 30 days of employment. Completion is tracked automatically
by the LMS.

Employees who fail to complete training by the deadline will have their access to Tier 3
and Tier 4 systems suspended until training is completed. Repeated non-compliance will
be escalated to HR for formal performance management action.

By continuing employment at Meridian Analytics Group, employees acknowledge that they
have read, understood, and agree to comply with this Data Governance Policy and all
associated standards and procedures.
