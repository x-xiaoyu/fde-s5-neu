MERIDIAN ANALYTICS GROUP — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Section 4: Time Off and Leave Policies
Last Revised: January 2025

4.1 Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 15 days (120 hours) of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year.
Employees in their first year accrue PTO at a rate of 1.25 days per month beginning on the
first day of employment. PTO is available to use as it accrues; negative balances are not
permitted except with written manager approval.

Part-time employees who work at least 20 hours per week accrue PTO on a prorated basis
calculated against the 40-hour standard work week. Contractors and temporary staff are not
eligible for PTO benefits regardless of duration of engagement.

4.2 PTO Carryover
Unused PTO up to a maximum of 80 hours (10 days) may be carried into the following calendar
year. Any unused PTO in excess of 80 hours is forfeited on December 31st. Employees who are
unable to use PTO due to a medical leave of absence may apply for a one-time carryover
extension of up to 40 additional hours with HR approval. PTO carryover extensions must be
requested in writing no later than November 15th of the accrual year.

Employees who are involuntarily terminated will receive a payout of all accrued, unused PTO
at their current hourly rate. Employees who resign voluntarily will receive payout only for
accrued PTO up to the 80-hour carryover cap; PTO accrued beyond that cap is forfeited
upon resignation.

4.3 Requesting Time Off
PTO requests must be submitted through the Workday HR portal no fewer than 5 business days
in advance for requests of 3 days or fewer, and no fewer than 14 business days in advance for
requests of more than 3 days. Managers must respond to PTO requests within 3 business days
of submission. Silence does not constitute approval.

In cases of urgent personal situations, employees may submit a same-day request with manager
notification by phone or direct message. The formal Workday submission must still be completed
by the end of the same business day.

During designated company blackout periods (typically the last two weeks of December and the
final week of each fiscal quarter), PTO requests are subject to additional manager discretion
and may be denied due to business need. Blackout periods are published on the HR intranet
by September 1st each year.

4.4 Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days (80 hours) of sick leave per year, credited in full on January 1st.
Sick leave does not carry over and is not paid out upon termination. Sick leave may be used
for personal illness, medical appointments, mental health days, or to care for an immediate
family member who is ill.

For absences of more than 3 consecutive days, employees must notify their manager and HR by
the start of the fourth day. A note from a licensed medical professional may be required at
manager discretion for absences of more than 5 consecutive days.

New employees hired after July 1st receive a prorated sick leave balance of 40 hours for
the remainder of the calendar year.

4.5 Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to up to 5 paid days of bereavement leave for the death of an
immediate family member (spouse or domestic partner, child, parent, or sibling) and up to
3 paid days for extended family (grandparents, grandchildren, in-laws, and aunts or uncles).
Bereavement leave does not count against PTO or sick leave balances.

Employees may request up to 5 additional days of unpaid bereavement leave with manager and
HR approval. In exceptional circumstances, further accommodation may be arranged on a
case-by-case basis by the HR Business Partner assigned to the employee's department.

Bereavement leave is available from the first day of employment with no waiting period.
Documentation such as an obituary or memorial program may be requested but is not required
at the time of leave; employees have 30 days to provide documentation if requested.

4.6 Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for 16 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth,
adoption, or foster placement of a child. Secondary caregivers are eligible for 6 weeks of
paid parental leave. Leave must begin within 12 months of the qualifying event. Employees
must have been employed for at least 6 months to be eligible for paid parental leave;
employees with less than 6 months of tenure may take unpaid FMLA leave if eligible.

Parental leave runs concurrently with any applicable FMLA entitlement. Employees are
encouraged to discuss their return-to-work plan with HR and their manager at least 4 weeks
before their expected return date.

4.7 Jury Duty and Civic Leave
Employees called for jury duty will receive their full regular pay for up to 15 business days.
Jury duty beyond 15 days will be handled on a case-by-case basis. Employees must notify their
manager and HR as soon as they receive a jury summons and provide a copy of the summons.
Employees are expected to report to work on any day or portion of a day they are released
from jury duty before 3:00 PM.

Employees who wish to vote in federal, state, or local elections are entitled to up to
2 hours of paid civic leave if their work schedule does not allow reasonable time to vote
outside of work hours.
